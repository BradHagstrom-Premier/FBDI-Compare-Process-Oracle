# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal Definian Oracle integration consultants. They receive this report after each Oracle Cloud quarterly release, read field-level changes tab by tab, and act on them inside client Applaud installations. They are technical — comfortable with database column names, data types, and positional numbering. They need to move through the report efficiently and translate what they read directly into action.

## Product Purpose

The FBDI Compliance Report surfaces the exact field-level differences between two Oracle Cloud FBDI template releases — added columns, removed columns, type changes, position shifts — mapped to the Applaud target tables that consultants maintain for clients. The report is the authoritative action list for each quarterly Oracle release cycle. Success means a consultant can open the report, understand the full scope of required changes, and close it with a clear work list — no cross-referencing other documents.

## Brand Personality

Definian Data Design. Modern, tech-forward, precise. The brand projects trust and clarity through structure, not decoration. The visual system earns confidence by being exact — tight alignment, purposeful hierarchy, nothing superfluous.

## Anti-references

None specified. Full creative latitude within the Definian palette.

## Design Principles

1. **Data leads, decoration follows.** Every visual element must earn its place by serving comprehension. If removing it would make the content clearer, remove it.
2. **Two surfaces, two truths.** The HTML report is designed for screen: task list, interactive, collapsible, spatially generous. The PDF is designed for print: static, compact, legible at 100%. They do not need to look alike — each should be the best version of itself in its medium. The HTML can be used by consultants to update and track changes in their respective client Applaud systems. The PDF gives the official what changed and how does it relate to Applaud that one can easily read.
3. **Color as signal, not style.** The palette's job is semantic: green for additions and Financials, red for removals, amber for modifications, the brand blue for structure and hierarchy. Color should be predictable enough that a consultant can glance and orient without reading.
4. **Trust through precision.** The report's authority comes from exactness — tabular alignment, consistent type scale, unambiguous change labels. Anything that looks approximate or decorative undermines the consultant's confidence in the data.
5. **Definian identity through structure.** Brand presence comes from the palette, the type discipline, and the document's confident organization — not from logos, flourishes, or cover drama.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target. Reasonable contrast and readability are sufficient. The PDF path is the primary formal deliverable; the HTML is the interactive companion. They may diverge significantly, slightly, or not at all in design approach, the end results and purposes are what matter.
